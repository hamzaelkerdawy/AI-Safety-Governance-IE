# AI Risk, Governance & Safety Portfolio

This repository documents a structured six-month professional development program focused on AI Risk Management, AI Governance, and Responsible AI practices, with a specific emphasis on non-coding, analytical, and auditing-oriented competencies.

The work in this repository reflects a practical application of industrial engineering principles (risk analysis, process control, audit, and governance) to modern AI systems, particularly large language models (LLMs).

## Scope

This portfolio covers:
- AI risk identification and assessment
- Model failure modes (hallucination, bias, overconfidence)
- AI governance frameworks (NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001)
- Responsible AI and AI safety principles
- AI red teaming (non-technical / strategic perspective)
- AI policy, compliance, and ethical risk analysis
- AI audit and assurance practices

## Why This Matters

AI systems increasingly influence high-impact decisions (hiring, credit, healthcare, content moderation). These systems introduce new forms of operational, ethical, legal, and reputational risk.

This portfolio demonstrates the ability to:
- Identify and classify AI risks
- Evaluate AI systems against regulatory and governance standards
- Detect failure modes such as hallucination, bias, and misalignment
- Translate technical behavior into business and compliance language
- Support organizations in deploying AI responsibly and safely


## Purpose
This repository demonstrates the ability to analyze AI systems, identify risks, and translate technical behavior into business, policy, and governance insights to support responsible and safe AI deployment.
